Questions, answered

A few things to know about Mova.

Straightforward answers before your first trip.

What is Mova?+

Mova is a mobility platform that brings rides, driver opportunities, and delivery experiences together in one place.

How does making an offer work?+

For supported trips, you can suggest a fare within Mova’s pricing boundaries and respond to offers from available drivers.

Can I choose a fixed-price ride?+

Yes. Fixed-price booking lets you see and agree to the trip price before your journey begins.

What is a metered ride?+

A metered ride calculates the fare from the actual journey using Mova’s metered pricing model.

How do I become a Driver?+

Visit the Drive page to learn about the onboarding process and register your interest.

Does Mova offer delivery?+

Mova Courier is being built for package delivery and courier opportunities through the same Mova platform.
