Ride Terms

Service-specific draft terms supplementing the General Terms for Mova Ride.

Nigeria Launch Draft v1 — Pending Final Legal Review Effective date: To be confirmed · Draft generated 15 September 2026

1. Mova Ride platform

Mova provides technology through which Riders create ride requests and eligible Drivers choose whether to respond to or accept opportunities. The Driver performs the underlying physical transportation service; Mova operates the platform.

2. Make an Offer

A Rider may propose a fare within supported Mova marketplace rules, including any applicable floors, ranges, or limits. An eligible Driver may accept or counter where supported. Mova provides the technology and rules for this exchange but does not compel either party to agree. Final contract-formation wording requires counsel review.

3. Fixed Price

Mova may calculate and display an upfront fare using route information, pricing parameters, marketplace conditions, and other applicable factors. The Rider chooses whether to request at that price and an eligible Driver chooses whether to accept the opportunity. Mova presenting or calculating a fare does not mean Mova physically performs transportation.

4. Metered Ride

Mova may show an estimate before a Metered Ride. The final fare is determined through the platform using validated journey information and applicable metered pricing rules, which may reflect distance, duration, and disclosed components. Route, distance, duration, and ETA estimates may differ from actual conditions.

5. Rider and Driver responsibilities

Riders should provide accurate pickup and destination information, act lawfully and respectfully, use reasonable care in a Driver’s vehicle, and meet payment obligations. Drivers are responsible, subject to applicable law and platform requirements, for lawful driving, licensing, vehicle operation, vehicle condition, traffic-law compliance, and professional conduct.

6. Cancellations and completion

Mova may provide cancellation, waiting, stop, receipt, rating, and support functionality. Any applicable cancellation or waiting charge will be disclosed through the platform or applicable policy; no amount is specified in this draft. Mova does not guarantee Driver availability or acceptance.
