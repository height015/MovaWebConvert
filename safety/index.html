COMPREHENSIVE MULTI-LAYERED SECURITY

Protected before, during, and after every trip.

1. Biometric Driver Screening

Drivers must complete a live facial recognition scan before logging on to ensure account ownership. Every driver undergoes government NIN validation, criminal background checks, and driving history verification.

BEFORE EVERY TRIP Zero Account Sharing

2. Active Telematics & AI RideCheck

Our intelligent backend monitors every ride in real-time. If a vehicle takes an unusual stationary stop, deviates significantly from the planned route, or registers sudden impact telemetry, an automated safety check is triggered.

DURING EVERY RIDE Real-time Telemetry

3. In-App Privacy & Virtual Masking

Your personal phone number is never revealed. Calls and text messages between riders and drivers are routed through encrypted virtual numbers to guarantee privacy even after the trip concludes.

PRIVACY FIRST Encrypted Numbers

4. Post-Trip Care & Incident Resolution

Our 24/7 dedicated Safety Command team conducts rapid trip investigation, liaison with local emergency services, and fair arbitration for any security or transit dispute.

INCIDENT CARE 24/7 Command Center

COMMUNITY SAFETY STANDARDS

The 3-point check before you enter a vehicle.

01 Verify the License Plate
Check that the vehicle's registration plate matches the exact plate number displayed in your Mova app.
02 Confirm the Driver's Photo & Name
Look at your driver's profile in the app and confirm the face matches the person behind the wheel.
03 Ask 'Who are you picking up?'
Let the driver say your name before entering, and confirm your destination together.
04 Fasten Your Seatbelt
Buckle up in the back or front seat for your safety throughout the trip.
