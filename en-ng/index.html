FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Mova.

Clear, transparent answers about our pricing models, driver partnership, safety standards, and launch timeline.

What makes Mova different from traditional ride-hailing apps?+

Mova is built on radical transparency and rider choice. Unlike closed platforms that impose arbitrary surge algorithms, Mova gives you three distinct pricing options for every trip: propose an Offer, lock in a Fixed Upfront rate, or ride Metered. For drivers, we charge an industry-low 10% commission compared to the 25%+ charged elsewhere.

How does the "Make an Offer" ride model work?+

For supported routes, you can suggest a fare within Mova’s calibrated pricing boundaries. Nearby vetted drivers review your offer in real time and can accept directly or propose a counteroffer, ensuring a mutually agreed fare before booking.

How does Fixed Price protect riders from surge spikes?+

Fixed Price calculates a guaranteed upfront fare before you confirm your request. Once booked, that price is 100% locked. Heavy Lagos traffic, sudden rain, or route detours will never increase the agreed fare.

What is a Metered Ride?+

A Metered Ride uses calibrated GPS telemetry to compute your fare based on actual distance travelled and elapsed trip time, ideal when you have multiple stops or an unpredictable schedule.

How do drivers benefit from Mova's 10% commission?+

Drivers keep 90% of every fare they complete. In addition, Mova offers complete trip destination transparency before acceptance, direct weekly Monday bank payouts, and zero hidden device or platform rental fees.

What safety features protect riders and drivers during active trips?+

Every active trip features in-app SOS emergency dispatch, one-tap live GPS route sharing via WhatsApp or SMS, 24/7 dedicated telephone support via our Safety Command Center (+234 704 783 8642), and thorough 45-point vehicle and driver background vetting.
