Nigeria Launch Draft v1

Privacy Policy

Draft explanation of how Mova may handle personal information across its platform ecosystem.

Nigeria Launch Draft v1 — Pending Final Legal Review Effective date: To be confirmed · Draft generated 15 September 2026

1. Scope and controller

This Nigeria Launch Draft describes information handling for the public website, Mova applications, platform services, support, Safety functionality, and related ecosystem features. The controller/operator is [MOVA LEGAL ENTITY], [MOVA REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Privacy contact: [MOVA PRIVACY EMAIL]. This document requires review against the Nigeria Data Protection Act and applicable requirements.

2. Information Mova may process

Depending on the service, Mova may process account and identity information, contact and profile information, Rider, Driver, and Courier information, location and trip information, delivery and Recipient information, payment or transaction records, communications, ratings and feedback, Safety and incident information, support information, device and technical information, and fraud or security information. Mova should not collect more information than the relevant purpose requires.

3. Location information

Mova’s mobile applications may process precise location when needed for supported pickup, matching, trip or delivery execution, route/status information, Safety, fraud/security, or support functions, subject to device permissions, app role, product state, and applicable law. The public marketing website does not require precise browser geolocation. [BACKGROUND LOCATION RULES — CONFIRM AGAINST FINAL MOBILE IMPLEMENTATION].

4. Trip, delivery, and Recipient information

Trip information may include pickup, destination, route, timestamps, ride mode, fare, status, cancellation, and completion. Delivery information may include Sender, pickup, Recipient details, destination, Courier, status, timestamps, route, and completion. A Sender may provide a Recipient’s name, phone number, destination, or other information needed to arrange delivery. Existing Mova Recipients may receive supported delivery participation; external Recipients do not automatically receive an account.

5. How Mova may use information

Mova may use information to provide platform services, manage accounts, match users, support rides and deliveries, calculate or display pricing, facilitate payments, send communications, provide Safety and support, prevent fraud and security incidents, comply with law, improve reliability where lawful, and exercise or defend legal rights. Final legal bases require counsel review and may include contract, legal obligations, legitimate interests, consent where required, and protection of vital interests where applicable.

6. Sharing information

Information may be shared as needed among matched Riders, Drivers, Senders, Couriers, and Recipients; with communications, payment, cloud, security, support, and infrastructure providers; with professional advisers; with authorities where legally required; and with business-transfer counterparties where applicable. Mova should not expose more personal information than the relevant platform purpose requires. Provider categories may change by country.

7. Communications and security

Mova may send account, OTP/security, trip, delivery, support, Safety, and transactional communications through push notifications, SMS, email, or in-app channels where supported. Mova uses technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, but no system can promise absolute security. Mova may process information to protect accounts, detect fraud, prevent abuse, and investigate suspicious activity.

8. Retention and deletion

Mova retains information for as long as reasonably needed for service, account, legal, fraud/security, financial recordkeeping, dispute, Safety, and limitation-period purposes. [DATA RETENTION SCHEDULE — TO BE CONFIRMED]. Users may request account deletion, subject to lawful retention and other applicable obligations. Final account-deletion behavior must be compared with the mobile applications.

9. Privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, complain, and exercise other statutory rights. Mova may verify identity before acting. Requests should be directed to [MOVA PRIVACY EMAIL]. Users may also have the right to complain to the applicable Nigerian data-protection authority.

10. Children and changes

The platform is not intended for people below [MINIMUM USER AGE], except where a specific approved product flow provides otherwise and applicable law permits it. Mova may update this Policy with appropriate notice. Final review must cover permissions, background location, push tokens, device identifiers, analytics or crash tools, payments, account deletion, international transfers, retention, and Recipient behavior in the completed mobile applications.
