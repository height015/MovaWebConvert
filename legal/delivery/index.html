Delivery & Courier Terms

Service-specific draft terms for Senders, Recipients, and Couriers using Mova Delivery.

Nigeria Launch Draft v1 — Pending Final Legal Review Effective date: To be confirmed · Draft generated 15 September 2026

1. Delivery platform role

Mova provides technology through which Senders create delivery requests and independent Couriers choose whether to accept and perform them. Unless Mova expressly identifies a service as direct, Mova does not itself collect, carry, transport, or deliver the parcel. The Courier performs the underlying physical delivery service.

2. Sender responsibilities

Senders are responsible for accurate pickup and recipient information, lawful parcel contents, appropriate packaging, accurate descriptions where requested, reasonable access for collection, and payment obligations. Senders should not provide unnecessary personal information about a Recipient.

3. Recipient participation

Where a Recipient’s phone number corresponds to an existing Mova account, Mova may associate that user with the active delivery for supported participation, such as in-app notifications, delivery visibility, Courier information, progress, and completion information. These capabilities depend on the product and applicable rules.

4. External Recipients

A Recipient without a Mova account does not automatically become a registered Mova user merely because a Sender provides contact information. Mova may process that information only as permitted for arranging delivery, collection or handoff, communications, Safety, support, legal, and security purposes. An external Recipient is not casually treated as having accepted the full Mova Terms.

5. Courier responsibilities

Couriers are responsible, subject to applicable law and platform requirements, for lawful operation, appropriate equipment or vehicle, reasonable parcel care, pickup verification where supported, delivery to the intended Recipient or location, professional conduct, Safety, and accurate completion information. Courier independence does not remove Mova’s responsibilities for the platform it operates.

6. Parcel rules

Senders must not request delivery of illegal goods, stolen property, prohibited weapons or explosives, controlled or illegal drugs, dangerous or hazardous substances, human remains, living persons, or other items prohibited by law or Mova policy. Regulated goods may require permits or special handling. [HIGH-VALUE / EXCLUDED ITEMS POLICY — TO BE CONFIRMED].

7. Pricing, tracking, and completion

Mova may facilitate pricing, communications, delivery status, tracking, notifications, and completion records. Pricing and delivery estimates are displayed through the platform where appropriate and are not guaranteed. Tracking may be unavailable or inaccurate in some circumstances.

8. Delivery issues

Users should report non-delivery, wrong-recipient, damage, or other disputes through available Mova support. Final rules for returns, redelivery, refunds, insurance, declared value, and loss or damage liability remain unresolved: [RETURN / REDELIVERY POLICY — TO BE CONFIRMED], [MAXIMUM PARCEL VALUE / LIABILITY POLICY — TO BE CONFIRMED], [DELIVERY LOSS / DAMAGE LIABILITY TERMS — COUNSEL AND BUSINESS TO CONFIRM], and [INSURANCE POSITION — BUSINESS/COUNSEL TO CONFIRM].
